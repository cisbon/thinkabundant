Coaching

Future-proofing, made concrete

A guided engagement that takes a leadership team from "we should probably do something about AI" to a shared five-year picture and a roadmap with owners and dates.

Who it is for

  • Owners and directors of small and mid-sized businesses
  • Leadership teams facing fast-moving technological change
  • Family businesses planning a handover across a long horizon
  • Teams that have run a strategy day and want something that survives contact with Monday

What you get

  • A documented view of your current business model
  • A mapped landscape of the forces shaping your industry
  • An articulated five-year vision and positioning
  • A backcast roadmap of the steps that get you there

How it runs

  1. Intake call. Scope, participants and the questions worth answering.
  2. Working sessions. The six steps below, run with your team rather than presented to it.
  3. Roadmap handover. Documented outputs your team can act on and revisit.
  4. Follow-up. Periodic reviews as the landscape moves.

Methodology

The six steps

Each step feeds the next: you cannot position for a future you have not described, and you cannot describe one without first mapping what is driving it.

  1. 1

    Business Model Canvas

    What does the business model look like today?

    The honest starting point. Before any foresight work, the team agrees on how the business actually creates, delivers and captures value right now.

  2. 2

    Map of Future Forces

    (Circular Model, that goes in more and more detail. e.g. at the first it can just be a circle out of two technologies: AI and Robotics → and then we go into more detail)

    The nesting, from broad to specific:

    • Which technologies?
      • Which services/products?
        • Which organizations?
          • Which individuals?

    Which 20 individuals/organizations will influence my industry in 5 years?

  3. 3

    Future Vision of the Industry (5-Year Horizon)

    What the mapped forces add up to: a described picture of the industry five years out.

  4. 4

    Ideal Positioning (5-Year Horizon)

    Where your business should stand inside that picture — and what makes that position defensible.

  5. 5

    Backcasting

    Working backwards from the ideal position to today, identifying the milestones that have to be true along the way.

  6. 6

    Roadmap

    The backcast turned into sequenced, owned actions with dates — the document the team actually works from.
